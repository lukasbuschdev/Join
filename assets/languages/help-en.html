Help

Welcome to the help page for Join. Here you can learn more about the system, its purpose, its main features, and how to use the different tools available within the platform.

What is Join?

Join is a Kanban-based project management system developed by Servicios Web Mérida as an example of a custom internal productivity tool and web-based business system.

The project demonstrates how a tailored web application can help teams organize their work, manage projects, collaborate with other users, assign responsibilities, and track tasks through different stages of completion.

Join is based on the principles of Kanban, a visual project management method used to organize workflows and make the current status of tasks easy to understand. Tasks can move between to do, in progress, awaiting feedback, and done, providing teams with a clear overview of their work.

In addition to task and board management, Join includes functionality such as user accounts, email verification, contact and collaborator management, live notifications, board invitations, subtasks, drag & drop interactions, and multilingual support.

Join serves primarily as a demonstration project and portfolio example for custom web system development. It is not operated as a commercial project management service, and continuous availability or production-level service guarantees are therefore not provided.

How to Use It

Here is a step-by-step guide to using Join:

  • Registration & Verification

    When registering a new account with Join, after entering a username, email address, and password and confirming the privacy policy, a verification email is sent. To confirm the account, you can click on the link in the email or enter and submit the verification code manually or via copy & paste on the verification page.

  • Profile Customization

    It is possible to upload or delete a profile picture and choose a profile color.

  • Login

    Users can log in to their account with the option to save their login data.

  • Reset Password

    Password resets are handled through email verification in a similar way to the account verification process.

  • Automatic Logout

    Users are automatically logged out after 5 minutes of inactivity.

  • Notifications & Websocket Server

    When sending a contact request, collaborator invitation, or assigning a task to a collaborator, the recipient receives a live notification if they are online. A WebSocket server has been implemented to provide this functionality.

  • Account Settings

    Clicking on your profile at the top right opens the account settings. Here you can view notifications, accept or decline contact requests and board invitations, reset your password, or delete your account. You can also log out from here.

  • Languages

    The language is initially loaded according to the default language configured in the browser. Language settings are also available during login and within the account settings.

  • Create a Board

    Newly created accounts do not have a default board. You must first create a board or be invited to an existing one. When creating a board, you can set its name, define categories, and invite collaborators.

  • Edit a Board

    After creation, a board can be edited by its creator. Collaborators can be added through invitations, and categories can be added or deleted. Collaborators currently cannot be removed after being added.

  • Delete a Board

    Entire boards can also be deleted after confirming the deletion.

  • Select a Board

    To select the active board, first click on "Overview"/"Summary" and then, if necessary, click on "Select Board". You can then select the desired board from the list.

  • Create Tasks

    Individual tasks, including subtasks, can be created and assigned to collaborators of the corresponding board.

  • Edit/Delete Tasks

    Existing tasks can be edited or deleted.

  • Move Tasks

    Tasks can be moved between the four workflow stages to do, in progress, awaiting feedback, and done via drag & drop in the board view.

  • Contact & Collaborator Requests

    When adding a contact or inviting a collaborator, a request is sent to the corresponding user. The request must be confirmed by the recipient before the user is added to the contacts list or board.

  • Add Contacts

    To add a new contact, at least part of a user's name must be entered in the search field before search results are displayed. When adding a contact, a contact request is sent to the user, which must be confirmed or declined.

Effective use of Join requires regular updates from you and your team so that the board reflects the current state of your project. If you have additional questions about Join, feel free to contact us at contacto@servicioswebmerida.com. We are happy to help.
